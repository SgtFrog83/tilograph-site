Every zoom is a real zoom.

Pinch towards any patch of the canvas and the pictures there come up sharp, with their neighbours around them. No list, no album to open first.

Video

Videos live on the canvas too.

They play where they sit as you pass over them. Open one and you get real frames to scrub, slow motion, a loop, and any frame saved as a picture.

Open

Tap a picture and it grows out of its own tile.

Swipe through its neighbours. Let it go and it settles back exactly where it was on the canvas.

Files

Folders become places.

Hand over a folder from iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox. Folders become places on the canvas, documents become tiles.

Make

Pick a few. Get a poster.

Choose pictures straight off the canvas and the app frames them as one poster. Square, story or portrait; reorder, round the corners, choose a look. Print it or share it.

Find

Type “beach 2019” and watch them light up.

Faces, subjects, places and colours are read on your device. Matches light where they sit, so you see when and where they belong. Lookalikes and duplicates are found for you.

Make it yours

Dark, medium or light. Or a look of your own.

The ground and its pattern, the accent, the panels, the buttons: every colour is yours. Keep up to five looks under their own names, a tap away.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your phone.

Faces, subjects and places are read on the device with Apple's own frameworks. No account, no server, no analytics. The whole policy fits on one page.
