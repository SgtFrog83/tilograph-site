TILOGRAPH

Your pictures, videos and files, tiled on a canvas.

Get it on the App Store
One canvas

Pinch out and see years at once. Pinch in and you are inside one afternoon. No list, no album to open first.

Arranged your way

By date, place, person, subject, colour, likeness or album. One tap, and the canvas lays itself out again.

Find anything

Type “beach 2019”, “dog” or a place. Matches light up where they sit, so you see when and where they belong.

Tidy up

Lookalikes and duplicates found for you. Compare, keep the best, send the rest to Recently Deleted.

Files too

Hand over a folder from iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox. Folders become places, documents become tiles.

Private by design

Faces, subjects and places are read on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. There is no account.

Make it yours

Dark, medium or light. Or a look of your own.

The ground and its pattern, the accent, the panels, the buttons: every colour is yours to set. Keep a look under its own name and it sits beside the three that come with the app, a tap away. Up to five custom themes, and every change can be undone, step by step.

Free for your most recent month, one poster and one folder. One purchase unlocks the full app for good, or take it by the year.
