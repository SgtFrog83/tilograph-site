See

Get closer, and closer.

Every zoom is a real zoom. Pinch towards a patch of the canvas and the pictures there come up sharp; tap one and it grows out of its own tile to fill the screen. Swipe through its neighbours. Let it go and it settles back exactly where it was.

Video

Videos live on the canvas too.

They play where they sit as you pass over them. Open one and you get a scrubber with real frames, slow motion, a loop, a range, and a frame saved as a picture.

Find

Type “beach 2019” and watch them light up.

Faces, subjects, places and colours are read on your device. A search lights the matches where they sit, so you see when and where they belong, not just that they exist. Lookalikes and duplicates are found for you; compare them and keep the best.

Make

Pick a few. Get a poster.

Choose pictures straight off the canvas and the app frames them as one poster. Square, story or portrait; reorder, round the corners, choose a look. Print it or share it.

Files

Folders become places.

Hand over a folder from iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox. Folders become places on the canvas, documents become tiles, and a tap opens any of them.

Make it yours

Dark, medium or light. Or a look of your own.

The ground and its pattern, the accent, the panels, the buttons: every colour is yours. Keep up to five looks under their own names, a tap away. Every change can be undone.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your phone.

Faces, subjects and places are read on the device with Apple's own frameworks. There is no account, no server and no analytics. The whole policy fits on one page.
